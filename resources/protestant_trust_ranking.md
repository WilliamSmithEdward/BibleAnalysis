# Protestant Trust Priority Ranking

This ranks only the 14 `.txt` files downloaded into `C:\Bible\resources`.

Score meaning: `0` is least trusted or least useful as a Protestant authority source; `100` is highest priority of trust in broad Protestant use. A lower score does not mean the text is worthless or malicious. It usually means the translation is niche, derivative, non-Protestant, incomplete for Christian canon use, or not widely received in Protestant churches.

Modern copyrighted translations such as ESV, NASB, NIV, CSB, NKJV, and NLT are not included because they were not part of the downloaded public text set.

| Rank | Score | File | Translation | Protestant trust rationale | Best use |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 95 | `kjv.txt` | King James Version | The strongest historical Protestant reception in this set. It was the standard English Bible for centuries and remains deeply trusted in many conservative Protestant circles. Its limits are archaic English and an older textual base. | Tradition, memorization, comparison, public-domain baseline |
| 2 | 88 | `asv.txt` | American Standard Version | A respected 1901 formal revision in the KJV/Revised Version tradition and an important ancestor of later Protestant translations. Old style, but strong pedigree. | Formal public-domain study |
| 3 | 84 | `web.txt` | World English Bible | A modern public-domain Bible based on the ASV, with Protestant 66-book editions available. Good practical trust for open digital use, though less church-wide adoption than KJV/ASV. | Modern public-domain reading and software use |
| 4 | 80 | `bsb.txt` | Berean Standard Bible | A modern Protestant-friendly translation with transparent public-facing materials and Bible Hub/Berean ecosystem support. Trust is good, but its reception history is much shorter than KJV/ASV. | Clear modern reading and study |
| 5 | 76 | `erv.txt` | English Revised Version | Historically important Protestant/Anglican revision tradition, closely related to the ASV. Less common today, but still a serious historical translation. | Historical comparison with KJV/ASV |
| 6 | 69 | `dbt.txt` | Darby Bible Translation | A literal translation associated with John Nelson Darby and Brethren/dispensational circles. Respected in a niche, not broadly used across Protestant churches. | Literal comparison, dispensational/Brethren study |
| 7 | 66 | `ylt.txt` | Young's Literal Translation | Valued as a very literal study aid, but awkward for ordinary reading and not a normal church Bible. Trusted more as a tool than as a primary translation. | Literal-language checking |
| 8 | 58 | `akjv.txt` | American King James Version | KJV-adjacent and familiar in style, but with limited independent church or scholarly reception compared with the KJV itself. | KJV-style reading with minor modernization |
| 9 | 54 | `wbt.txt` | Webster's Bible Translation | A historical KJV revision by Noah Webster. Useful, but broad Protestant authority and adoption are limited. | Historical KJV-family comparison |
| 10 | 52 | `jps.txt` | JPS Tanakh 1917 OT / Weymouth NT | This downloaded Open Bible file pairs the respected Jewish Publication Society 1917 Old Testament with the Weymouth New Testament. Useful for comparison, but not a Protestant-first committee Bible and not widely used as a primary church text. | OT/NT historical comparison |
| 11 | 50 | `brb.txt` | Berean Reader's Bible | Related to the Berean translation family and useful for readability, but as a reader-oriented text it has less priority than the BSB for reference or study. | Smooth reading |
| 12 | 43 | `slt.txt` | Smith's Literal Translation | A niche literal translation with limited broad Protestant adoption. Useful for comparison, not a primary trust source. | Secondary literal comparison |
| 13 | 25 | `drb.txt` | Douay-Rheims Bible | Historically significant Roman Catholic translation from the Latin Vulgate tradition. Protestants may consult it comparatively, but it is not a Protestant authority source. | Catholic-tradition comparison |
| 14 | 18 | `cpdv.txt` | Catholic Public Domain Version | A modern Catholic public-domain text with little broad Protestant reception. Lowest Protestant priority in this set, though still useful for comparison. | Catholic public-domain comparison |

## Practical Priority

For a Protestant-first public-domain corpus, prioritize:

1. `kjv.txt`
2. `asv.txt`
3. `web.txt`
4. `bsb.txt`

For literal comparison, add:

5. `dbt.txt`
6. `ylt.txt`
7. `slt.txt`

For historical or non-Protestant comparison, keep but do not treat as Protestant-first:

8. `jps.txt`
9. `drb.txt`
10. `cpdv.txt`
