# Jesus As Home

Theme: Jesus as safety, rest, belonging, future, and the place where every good thing is found.

## Why This Study Exists

The user said:

> Jesus is my home.

And clarified:

> Everything that is good in my life is found in my relationship with Jesus.

In this workspace, "home" means safety, rest, peace, protection, love, family, and the place where a person can finally be themself. This study asks how Scripture speaks to that reality.

## Anchor 1: Jesus Speaks To Troubled Hearts

John 14:1, BSB:

```text
Do not let your hearts be troubled. You believe in God; believe in Me as well.
```

Jesus begins the home passage by speaking directly to troubled hearts.

That matters. The promise of home is not given to people who already feel stable. It is given to disciples facing confusion, loss, and the coming violence of the cross. Jesus does not treat troubled hearts as an embarrassment. He speaks to them.

The first movement toward home is trust:

- believe in God
- believe also in Jesus

This fits the user's faith orientation: faith is agreeing with the truth about God's character, who He is, what He values, and what He has personally done through the cross.

## Anchor 2: The Father's House Has Room

John 14:2, BSB:

```text
In My Father's house are many rooms. If it were not so, would I have told you that I am going there to prepare a place for you?
```

Across the corpus, the translations use images like:

- many mansions
- many homes
- many rooms
- many abodes
- many dwellings
- many resting-places

The shared meaning is generous space in the Father's house.

For someone who longs for security, this is not only architecture language. It means there is room with God. Jesus does not describe the Father's house as cramped, grudging, or barely available. He says there are many rooms, and He is preparing a place.

Home with God is not accidental. It is prepared.

## Anchor 3: Home Is Being Where Jesus Is

John 14:3, BSB:

```text
And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come back and welcome you into My presence, so that you also may be where I am.
```

This is the heart of the study.

Jesus does not only promise a place. He promises presence:

```text
so that you also may be where I am
```

The prepared place matters because Jesus is there. The Father's house is home because it is life with Him.

This connects directly to the user's statement that Jesus is home. Home is not merely a future location. Home is relationship with Jesus, presence with Jesus, and belonging to Jesus.

## Anchor 4: Jesus Is The Way Home

John 14:6, BSB:

```text
Jesus answered, "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me."
```

Jesus does not merely give directions to home. He is the way.

This matters because human beings often try to find home through self-rule, performance, achievement, distraction, control, or other people. But Jesus says the way to the Father is Himself.

He is:

- the way
- the truth
- the life

Home is not found apart from truth. Home is not found apart from life. Home is not found apart from Jesus.

## Anchor 5: Dwelling In The House Of The Lord

Psalm 23:6, BSB:

```text
Surely goodness and mercy will follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the LORD forever.
```

Psalm 23 ends with dwelling.

The path includes dark valleys, enemies, and need for restoration. Yet the final direction is not catastrophe. It is the house of the LORD.

This speaks to the user's need for reassurance that life will not end in catastrophe. Psalm 23 does not deny the valley. It says goodness and mercy follow, and the destination is dwelling with God.

## Anchor 6: One Thing Desired

Psalm 27:4, BSB:

```text
One thing I have asked of the LORD; this is what I desire: to dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to gaze on the beauty of the LORD and seek Him in His temple.
```

Psalm 27 makes dwelling with God the central desire.

The point is not only safety from danger. It is beholding the beauty of the LORD. Home is where God is known, seen, sought, and enjoyed.

This connects with the user's belief that Scripture steers a person toward knowing who God is as a person, what He values, and relational knowing.

## Anchor 7: No Longer Strangers

Ephesians 2:19, BSB:

```text
Therefore you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints and members of God's household,
```

In Christ, believers are no longer strangers.

This is home language at the level of identity. A person does not merely visit God's household. They belong there.

For someone who needs security, the word "household" matters. It means family, citizenship, belonging, and shared life. The church is not first an institution. It is a people who have eternal relationship with God.

## Anchor 8: God Makes His People His Dwelling

Ephesians 2:22, BSB:

```text
And in Him you too are being built together into a dwelling place for God in His Spirit.
```

The home theme moves in both directions:

- God's people dwell with God.
- God dwells with His people.

This is astonishing. Jesus prepares a place for His people, and by the Spirit God also makes His people a dwelling place for Himself.

Home is not only "I go to God." Home is also "God comes to dwell with us."

## Anchor 9: The Final Home

Revelation 21:3-4, BSB:

```text
Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man, and He will dwell with them. They will be His people, and God Himself will be with them as their God.
He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and there will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the former things have passed away.
```

The end of Scripture is not humanity escaping into abstraction. It is God dwelling with His people.

And where God fully dwells:

- tears are wiped away
- death is no more
- mourning is no more
- crying is no more
- pain is no more

This is the fullness of home.

Revelation 21:7, BSB:

```text
The one who overcomes will inherit all things, and I will be his God, and he will be My son.
```

The final home is relational: God and His children.

## Working Synthesis

Scripture presents home with God as:

1. A word spoken to troubled hearts.
2. A prepared place in the Father's house.
3. Being where Jesus is.
4. Coming to the Father through Jesus, who is the way, truth, and life.
5. Dwelling in the house of the LORD forever.
6. Seeing and seeking the beauty of the LORD.
7. No longer being a stranger, but belonging to God's household.
8. God dwelling with His people by the Spirit.
9. The final end of tears, death, mourning, crying, and pain.

The deepest point is this:

Home is not only a place Jesus prepares.

Home is Jesus Himself.

## Prayer

Jesus, be my home. When my heart is troubled, help me believe You. When I feel like a stranger, remind me that I belong in Your Father's house. When life feels chaotic, give me the security of knowing that You are preparing a place, and that the place is precious because You are there. Let every good thing in my life lead me back to You. Amen.
