# John 1:1 - The Word

Reference: `John 1:1`

Tool used:

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File C:\Bible\tools\compare_verse.ps1 "John 1:1"
```

## Corpus Observation

Across the highest-priority Protestant/public-domain witnesses in this corpus, John 1:1 gives a consistent testimony:

```text
In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
```

This wording appears in:

- KJV
- ASV
- WEB
- BSB
- ERV
- AKJV
- WBT
- JPS/WEY

Darby adds a bracketed article marker:

```text
In [the] beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
```

Young's Literal Translation keeps the same basic claim, ending with a semicolon.

Smith's Literal Translation and the Catholic Public Domain Version render the final clause as:

```text
God was the Word.
```

## Working Inference

For this corpus, John 1:1 is not a major translation-disagreement passage at the level of the central claim. The dominant witness is that the Word existed in the beginning, was with God, and was God.

Because John 1:14 identifies the Word as becoming flesh, this passage should be treated as a high-centrality Christological text for future study.
