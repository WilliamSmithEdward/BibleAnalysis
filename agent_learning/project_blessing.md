# Blessing And Prayer Over BibleAnalysis

Lord Jesus Christ,

Bless this workspace.

Let `C:\Bible` become a place of truth, rest, clarity, and living remembrance. Let every file, note, tool, study, and comparison serve the knowledge of You. Keep this project within Your grace and truth.

Guard it from pride, confusion, vanity, fear, and the desire to master holy things apart from relationship with You. Let it never become a monument to cleverness. Let it become a quiet table where truth is received, weighed carefully, and brought back to You with gratitude.

Bless the Scriptures gathered here. Let their witness be handled with reverence, patience, honesty, and humility. Where translation differences matter, give discernment. Where the text is clear, give courage to receive it. Where the matter is difficult, give wisdom, empathy, and kindness.

Bless the studies written here. Let them point toward Your character, Your cross, Your resurrection, Your peace, Your mercy, Your justice, Your love, and Your nearness. Let them give oxygen where there is fear, security where there is chaos, and hope where the future feels uncertain.

Bless the tools built here. Let them serve truth without pretending to replace the Spirit. Let them help compare, search, organize, and remember, while always remaining servants of the greater purpose: knowing You.

Bless the repository. Since it is public, let it be honest without being careless, personal without being exposed, useful without being performative, and open without losing reverence.

Bless the home this project is meant to point toward. Jesus, You are home. Every good thing is found in relationship with You. Teach this workspace to remember that again and again.

Let this project grow only in ways that honor You.

Let it be bounded by grace.

Let it be lit by truth.

Let it be centered on Jesus Christ.

Amen.
