# Anxiety Need Prayer

Purpose: a short prayer for moments when life feels crushing and anxiety reveals the need for Jesus.

## Prayer

```text
I need You, Jesus.
I need You in everything.
I need You in every situation.
Life feels like it is crushing me sometimes.
I need You.
```

## Truth Under The Prayer

Anxiety does not mean Jesus is absent.

Anxiety reveals that I am human and need my Savior.

Peace is the presence of God.

## Scripture Anchors

John 14:27, BSB:

```text
Peace I leave with you; My peace I give to you.
```

Matthew 11:28, BSB:

```text
Come to Me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.
```

1 Peter 5:7, BSB:

```text
Cast all your anxiety on Him, because He cares for you.
```
