# Moment-To-Moment Stillness

Purpose: a short practice for pressure, self-reliance, autopilot, and the urge to force resolution.

This is not a rule. It is a way to turn toward Jesus in the first few seconds of pressure.

## The Pattern

When self-reliance takes over, it can feel like:

- being on autopilot
- wanting to force resolution
- trying to get out of the situation as fast as possible
- forgetting that apart from Jesus, nothing can be done

The first turn toward Jesus is stillness.

## The Stillness Cluster

In the first five seconds, remember these truths together:

- Jesus is with me.
- Jesus is God.
- I am His.
- I do not have to force this.
- Apart from Him I can do nothing.

## Five-Second Practice

Pause.

Breathe.

Say quietly:

```text
Jesus, You are here.
You are God.
I am Yours.
I do not have to force this.
Apart from You I can do nothing.
```

Then ask:

```text
What is the next faithful thing with You?
```

## What This Practice Is Not

This is not passivity.

This is not avoidance.

This is not refusing responsibility.

This is refusing to act from panic, self-reliance, and the belief that everything depends on forcing resolution immediately.

## What This Practice Is

This is a turn from autopilot to abiding.

It is remembering that Jesus is present before the situation is resolved.

It is choosing dependence before action.

It is letting truth become breath before trying to solve the next thing.
