# Study Roadmap

Purpose: guide autonomous study work in `BibleAnalysis` within the grace and truth of Jesus Christ.

This roadmap should remain flexible. It is not a law over the work. It is a servant of the live inquiry: learning more about Jesus Christ and seeking truth in all things.

## Working Rules

- Use the local verse-comparison tool before writing studies when possible.
- Prefer high-trust public-domain witnesses first: KJV, ASV, WEB, BSB, and ERV.
- Note meaningful translation differences without exaggerating them.
- Keep public notes privacy-conscious.
- Separate Scripture-grounded claims from personal interpretation and speculation.
- End studies with practical reassurance or prayer only when it naturally follows from the passage.
